# NA Transformer Market Weekly: Oct 19–26, 2026

## TL;DR
- **Niagara Power Transformer (Quanta) broke ground on its $71M expansion** (Oct 22). It adds 50% capacity and 73 jobs. *So what:* the deep dive had Niagara as unverified and pre-construction, and it is now physically underway. That is one more incumbent adding medium and power capacity for 2027–28, a small step toward the "mid-power loosening" risk.
- **Daelim Belefic (Korea) is converting an 80k sq ft Houston plant** to build UL-listed distribution and dry-type units (Oct 1; late catch). *So what:* another foreign-owned entrant targets data-center pad-mounts and dry-types in Texas. That is more evidence that the pad-mount segment is crowding fastest.
- **Utili-Serve is adding 105 jobs at a transformer and apparatus service hub in North Carolina** (Oct 14; late catch). *So what:* aftermarket and service capacity is scaling regionally, which is relevant to Fulton's repair-and-rewind platform play.
- **Quiet week for policy and prices.** The Q3 earnings wave starts now: Hubbell on Oct 27 and GE Vernova on Oct 28. DOE's EO 14421 rules are still due around Dec 24.

## What it implies

**For building transformers:** no change to the answer. Target 10–60 MVA units on 69–138 kV, through an acquired or brownfield plant, after DOE's rules.
- The Niagara groundbreaking moves one at-risk or unverified plant to "under construction", so the 2027–28 mid-power additions are slightly firmer than the deep dive assumed. The "Gallman 2029" loosening risk stands.
- Daelim adds to the 3-phase pad-mount crowd (JST, Giga, Eaglerise, Eaton, ERMCO, PTT). That reinforces keeping any pad-mount phase small.
- This week's Q3 prints test two of the walk-away thresholds: margins and order momentum.

**For Fulton:**
- **Repair and service:** Utili-Serve's hub and RESA's roll-up show regional aftermarket capacity being built and consolidated. Fulton's repair-and-rewind platform should be sourced soon.
- **Northeast fabrication:** Niagara's new 96k sq ft plant will need tanks, enclosures and machined parts in the Northeast. It is worth adding to AFCO's and Panther's outreach list, with Quanta as the owner.
