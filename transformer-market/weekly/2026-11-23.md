# NA Transformer Market Weekly: Nov 16 – 23, 2026

## TL;DR
- **A new entrant plans a $327.7M heavy power transformer plant in Gaffney, South Carolina** (Nov 17). LIMiK Capital, a subsidiary of LIMiK International (which claims 25 years of large-transformer experience), will build a full plant: core cutting and stacking, coil winding, oil processing, assembly and factory acceptance testing. It plans 400 jobs and production in early 2028. *So what:* the first new large-power entrant since the deep dive. Together with GE Vernova Shreveport (Nov 10), Niagara and Hitachi South Boston, 2028 is getting crowded at the larger end too, not just in pad-mounts. It is also another plant competing for the same few hundred coil winders.
- **SEL (Schweitzer Engineering Laboratories) may start making transformers in Idaho** (Nov 19, proposal only): a former Kmart site in Lewiston with up to 600 workers making transformers and metal cabinets. *So what:* if it goes ahead, a well-capitalized grid-equipment maker would enter distribution or small power transformers. Weak source; watch.
- **Quiet week for policy, prices and deals.** No DOE rule yet under EO 14421 (due Dec 24). The Grid SCALED concept-paper deadline passed on Nov 20. The transformer PPI for September and October is still not retrieved.

## What it implies

**For building transformers:** the target product is unchanged (10–60 MVA on 69–138 kV), but the case for building a new plant from scratch gets weaker.
- **2028 is now crowded across sizes.** Announced 2028 additions now include LIMiK (large power, size unknown), GE Vernova Shreveport, Niagara, Hyosung Memphis, VTC Muscle Shoals and Hitachi South Boston. A new entrant shipping first units in 2028–29 would arrive into this wave. That strengthens the deep dive's preference for buying an existing plant (faster to market, existing qualifications) over a greenfield build.
- **Labor is the binding constraint.** LIMiK's 400 jobs and SEL's possible 600 add to the 4,388 announced jobs at the plants hiring in 2027–28, against about 3,600 coil winders nationally. Any plan should budget for training and wage premiums.
- **Watch LIMiK's product range.** If it targets 100+ MVA, it competes with Hitachi and Hyundai rather than the 10–60 MVA niche; if it targets medium power, it competes directly with the recommended niche.

**For Fulton:** no Fulton-specific news. LIMiK's Gaffney plant, about an hour from Charlotte, will need tanks, enclosures, machined parts and freight in the Carolinas, alongside Siemens Energy Charlotte and PTT Raeford. Worth adding to AFCO's, Panther's and Transend's outreach lists.
