# NA Transformer Market Weekly: Oct 12–19, 2026

## TL;DR
- **Hitachi Energy will build a C$479M transformer-insulation (pressboard) plant in London, Ontario** (Oct 16). It is the first pressboard line in the Americas for Hitachi, and is due at the end of 2028. *So what:* one of the input bottlenecks (large-unit insulation) gets new North American capacity, but it mainly serves Hitachi's own plants. Independent builders stay dependent on Weidmann and imports until then.
- **GE Vernova and Venture Global signed a grid-equipment framework** (Oct 15): transformers and gas-insulated switchgear for up to 16–21 LNG power islands, with the first purchase orders by the end of 2026. *So what:* another large buyer outside the utility channel is locking in slots two to three years ahead, which keeps power-transformer lead times long.
- **Korean makers keep winning data-center work. LS Electric took a $133M order for 345 kV units** delivered to nine substations through 2030 (reported Oct 3; late catch). *So what:* the EHV and data-center segment stays with incumbents and importers; nothing here changes the advice to avoid LPT/EHV.
- **The September producer price index came out Oct 15** (final demand +6.3% YoY). The transformer-specific series was not retrievable this week, so the re-acceleration call (+7.6–8.7% YoY in Jul–Aug) is still unconfirmed for September.

## What it implies

**For building transformers:** the deep dive's answer is unchanged. Build one size class up (10–60 MVA, 69–138 kV) through an acquired or brownfield plant, after DOE's EO 14421 rules (around Dec 24).
- The Hitachi pressboard plant is a reminder that incumbents are vertically integrating their scarce inputs. An entrant should secure insulation kits from two sources, such as Weidmann and an importer, before committing.
- Large non-utility buyers keep reserving slots years ahead: LNG power islands, and hyperscalers through Korean makers. That keeps substation and power-unit lead times long into 2028–29, which supports the plan to target that segment.

**For Fulton:**
- **Insulation:** the Hitachi plant makes the "independent insulation-kit supplier" play (ranked 8th, low conviction) slightly weaker for 2029 onward. It is still open until then.
- **Repair and service:** RESA's 3MD deal is another private-equity-backed roll-up of regional transformer service shops. It supports play #2 (a repair-and-rewind platform) and shows that competition for founder-owned shops is active. Move early.
