# NA Transformer Market Weekly: Nov 2 – 9, 2026

## TL;DR
- **Eaton's Q3 confirms the North American electrical cycle is still accelerating** (Nov 3). Electrical Americas backlog reached $16.0B (+33% YoY), rolling 12-month organic orders rose 39%, organic sales 22%, and the margin recovered to 29.8% (+230 bp sequentially). *So what:* together with GE Vernova, Hyosung and Quanta last week, the big order books are still growing. Hammond's sequential dip looks company-specific so far, not a turn.
- **ERMCO is doubling its Granby, Quebec plant** (~Oct; late catch): 3 new lines, 200 jobs, to ~200 units a month. *So what:* one more cooperative-owned distribution maker adding custom capacity, alongside its 500-job Arizona plant. Distribution supply keeps loosening faster than medium and large power.
- **Transformer prices have plateaued at a high level.** The transformer PPI (WPU1174) stepped up ~4% in June and was flat in July–August (434.3 to 434.5); electrical equipment manufacturing PPI kept edging up through September (256.3). *So what:* the first data point toward the deep dive's "PPI growth under 3% YoY" walk-away threshold for distribution; worth confirming with the October release.
- **Quiet week for policy and deals.** No EO 14421 rules yet (due ~Dec 24); DOE Grid SCALED concept papers are due Nov 20.

## What it implies

**For building transformers:** no change. Target 10–60 MVA units on 69–138 kV, via an acquired or brownfield plant, after DOE's EO 14421 rules.
- **Demand:** Eaton's backlog and orders, on top of last week's prints, keep the order side well ahead of shipments into 2027. No walk-away threshold is tripped.
- **Prices:** the flat July–August transformer PPI is the first sign that price increases are pausing. A new entrant should underwrite flat real prices from 2027, not continued 5–10% increases, especially in distribution.
- **Inputs:** a second domestic GOES producer (Big River, 2028) would loosen the core-steel bottleneck right as new plants ramp, and lower the cost of Section 232 duties on imported laminations. It is still only a target; watch U.S. Steel/Nippon disclosures.
- **Distribution is crowding:** ERMCO Granby and Arizona, Daelim Houston and JST/Giga keep adding pad-mount and custom distribution capacity. Keep any distribution phase small.

**For Fulton:** no Fulton-specific news. The Nov 20 Grid SCALED concept-paper deadline (from last week) is the near-term action.
