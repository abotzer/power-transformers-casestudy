# NA Transformer Market Weekly: Nov 9 – 16, 2026

## TL;DR
- **GE Vernova will spend $34.5M to expand its Shreveport, Louisiana transformer plant** (Nov 10): 175 new jobs on top of ~630, full capacity in 2028, aimed at industrial and renewable-energy transformers. *So what:* the first capacity move since GE Vernova took full ownership of Prolec GE. It adds medium-power supply for 2028, the same window where the deep dive flagged a risk of loosening.
- **Producer prices kept rising in October** (BLS, Nov 13): final demand +0.3% m/m and +6.5% YoY. *So what:* general inflation is not easing. The transformer-specific series (flat in July–August) has not been retrieved for September or October yet, so last week's "price plateau" is still unconfirmed.
- **Wood Mackenzie still expects a 15% power transformer shortage in 2026** (8% for substations), and sees EO 14421 worsening it above 100 MVA, where data centers had been using Chinese suppliers. *So what:* the large-unit gap remains open while distribution keeps filling.
- **Siemens Energy closed a record fiscal year** (to Sep 30): Grid Technologies orders +14.5%, revenue €44.2B, dividend more than tripled. *So what:* one more global supplier with full order books, relevant because Siemens Energy's Charlotte large-transformer plant starts in early 2027.
- **No movement on policy:** DOE's EO 14421 rules are still due Dec 24; Grid SCALED concept papers are due **Nov 20**.

## What it implies

**For building transformers:** no change. Target 10–60 MVA units on 69–138 kV through an acquired or brownfield plant, after DOE's EO 14421 rules.
- **Medium-power additions keep coming for 2028** (GE Vernova Shreveport, Niagara, Virginia Transformer Rincon, Hyosung Memphis). The 2028–29 window is where a new entrant would ship first units, so the "loosening by 2028–29" risk keeps rising. Underwrite against a narrower margin premium from 2028.
- **The large-unit gap is the most durable:** a 15% shortage, EO 14421 pressure on Chinese supply above 100 MVA, and lead times around 128 weeks. That argues for keeping a path to 100+ MVA in the plant design, even if the first products are 10–60 MVA.
- **Inflation is still running hot (PPI +6.5%)**, which supports pricing but also raises copper, steel and labor costs for any new plant budget.

**For Fulton:** no Fulton-specific news. **Grid SCALED concept papers are due this Friday, Nov 20.** If AFCO, Panther or Stainless Foundry (with a transformer OEM) or the repair-and-rewind play are to apply, this is the week.
