# NA Transformer Market Weekly: Oct 26 – Nov 2, 2026

## TL;DR
- **GE Vernova agreed to buy Trench Group for ~$9B** (Oct 28). Trench makes bushings, instrument transformers and coils at 11 plants; the deal closes late 2027. *So what:* the biggest component deal of the cycle. GE Vernova says it buys most of its bushings outside today, so one of the few merchant bushing suppliers becomes a rival's captive. Independent transformer builders should lock in bushing supply (Hitachi Alamo, Trench, PCORE, Chinese/Indian sources) before 2028.
- **Q3 prints say demand is intact, not accelerating everywhere.** GE Vernova Electrification orders were $5.4B (-9% organic on a tough comp, book-to-bill ~1.4) with backlog up 60% to $42.2B and a 19.1% EBITDA margin. Hammond's backlog fell 3.8% QoQ as shipments outran bookings, though still +61% YoY. Hyosung's backlog hit a record ₩18.9T. *So what:* pricing power holds at the top; Hammond's sequential dip is the first soft signal in dry-type and data-center magnetics and one of the walk-away indicators to keep watching.
- **DOE opened a $237M "Grid SCALED" call** (Oct 30) for US-made transformer subcomponents and materials ($187M) and refurbishment and reuse ($50M). Concept papers are due **Nov 20**. *So what:* a direct, near-term grant opening for component makers and for the repair-and-rewind play.
- **Late catch: LS Electric won a ~$139M order** (₩185.3B, ~Oct 13) for 38 kV switchgear and distribution transformers at a Bloom Energy-linked Pennsylvania data center. *So what:* Korean makers keep taking data-center distribution work, not only EHV units.

## What it implies

**For building transformers:** the core answer holds. Target 10–60 MVA units on 69–138 kV through an acquired or brownfield plant, after DOE's EO 14421 rules (~Dec 24).
- **Bushings become a strategic input.** With Trench moving inside GE Vernova (and Hitachi making its own in Alamo), the merchant bushing market for independents narrows from 2028. A new entrant should secure multi-year bushing supply early or plan a second source; this also makes a bushing or tap-changer component play more attractive.
- **Grant money for components is now real.** Grid SCALED's $187M for US-made subcomponents and materials fits exactly the bottlenecks the deep dive flagged (cores, insulation, bushings, tanks). A concept paper by Nov 20 is cheap optionality.
- **Demand is strong but the first sequential softening showed up** in Hammond's backlog. It does not trip the walk-away threshold (it is shipments rising, not orders falling), but it is the first data point pointing toward the "mid-power and dry-type loosening by 2028–29" risk.

**For Fulton:**
- **Grid SCALED (Nov 20 concept papers):** the $50M refurbishment and reuse pool fits the repair-and-rewind platform play, and the $187M subcomponent pool fits AFCO (tanks and enclosures), Panther (machined parts) and Stainless Foundry (castings) if paired with a transformer OEM. Worth a quick decision this month on whether to file.
- **Trench:** GE Vernova's in-sourcing of bushings could leave independents (Virginia Transformer, Niagara, PTT) looking for new component suppliers. A useful conversation opener for Panther and Stainless Foundry.
- No Fulton-specific news this week.
