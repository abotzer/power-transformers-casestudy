How fast demand is growing

Mobile data traffic, usage per phone, fixed wireless at home, and what forecasters expect from AI devices such as glasses, cars and robots. The test for the thesis is whether growth is speeding up or slowing down.

Where new capacity comes from

Operators add capacity three ways: more spectrum, more cell sites, and more bits per hertz from better technology. Wi-Fi carries much of the load indoors. The more the other levers can do, the less a new megahertz is worth.

Who holds the spectrum

Licensed mobile spectrum by holder and band, after the 2025-2026 sales. Low-band travels far and through walls, mid-band carries most 5G capacity, and high-band (millimetre wave) is fast over short distances.

Satellites that talk to phones

Direct-to-device service connects ordinary phones to satellites. It needs spectrum of its own or a carrier's, and a satellite beam covers a large area with a fixed amount of capacity, which limits what it can do in cities.
